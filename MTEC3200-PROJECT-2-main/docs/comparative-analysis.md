# Competitive Analysis: Diabetes Visit Preparation & Context Tracking Tools

## Project Goal

Help people with diabetes better understand and communicate the "why" behind their glucose data so they can have more productive conversations with their endocrinologist and healthcare team.

The primary problem is that many patients struggle to remember the context surrounding glucose patterns weeks or months later. Most existing diabetes apps focus heavily on numbers, while varying in their ability to capture the real-life events, decisions, and circumstances that explain those numbers.

# 1. Glooko

**Website:** https://glooko.com

## What It Does

Glooko aggregates data from compatible CGMs, insulin pumps, blood glucose meters, and health devices into a single platform. It also allows users to add contextual information such as food, insulin, medications, and activity, then share that information with their healthcare providers. 【1-b151f8】【2-48aa61】

- Designed specifically to support communication with healthcare teams. 【1-b151f8】【2-48aa61】
- Provides reports and trend analysis for appointments. 【1-b151f8】【2-48aa61】
- Supports remote monitoring and data sharing with clinicians. 【1-b151f8】

## Cons

- Primarily focused on data collection and reporting.
- Requires users to manually enter context if they want detailed explanations behind glucose trends. 【1-b151f8】
- Can feel clinical and data-heavy for users experiencing diabetes burnout.

## Comparison to Your Idea

**Similarities**

- Attempts to connect glucose data with daily events.
- Improves provider access to patient information.

**Differences**

- Focuses on data aggregation.
- Less emphasis on capturing the patient's personal story or memory of events.
- Does not specifically address the feeling of being "interrogated" during appointments.

# 2. Tidepool

**Website:** https://www.tidepool.org

## What It Does

Tidepool combines information from insulin pumps, CGMs, automated insulin delivery systems, and blood glucose meters into a unified dashboard that can be shared with care teams. 【3-e327d8】【4-7145b0】

## Pros

- Excellent device integration. 【3-e327d8】【4-7145b0】
- Makes diabetes data easier to review visually. 【3-e327d8】
- Allows patients to securely share data with providers and caregivers. 【3-e327d8】【5-87d55f】
- Helps healthcare visits focus on patterns rather than data transfer issues. 【3-e327d8】
- Maintains long-term historical records even when devices change. 【3-e327d8】

## Cons

- Relies heavily on numerical device data.
- Limited support for capturing emotions, stress, life events, or burnout.
- Patients still need to remember why certain glucose events happened.

## Comparison to Your Idea

**Similarities**

- Seeks to improve conversations between patients and healthcare providers.
- Uses historical information to explain trends.

**Differences**

- Focuses on data accessibility.
- Does not focus on documenting personal circumstances, challenges, or decision-making processes.
- Solves the "where is my data?" problem more than the "why did this happen?" problem.
